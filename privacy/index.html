NUMKB / MAC + WEB

Privacy policy

Privacy and help for NumKB’s Mac app and Web numeric keypad.

Updated 12 October 2026

01

Scope

This policy covers NumKB’s Mac app, its phone-browser keypad, and this website. No separate NumKB account is required. App Store purchases use your Apple Account.

02

Input and local connections

Key commands travel from your phone to your Mac over the local network. NumKB does not upload them to a developer server or save an input history. Accessibility permission allows NumKB to send the requested keystrokes to the active Mac app. The Web connection uses HTTP, not transport encryption; use a trusted private network.

03

Data on your devices

The Mac stores paired device names and identifiers, session credentials, origin, connection times and input sequence state to reconnect and prevent repeated input. Preferences such as language and port stay on the Mac. The browser stores a random device identifier in local storage and pairing information in tab session storage. A last-observed time is stored in the Mac Keychain to prevent restarting a trial by changing the clock.

04

Purchases and diagnostics

Apple processes payments and provides verified transaction records used to calculate the 7-day trial and lifetime Pro access. NumKB does not receive payment-card details. Local diagnostics may include service, connection and permission status. No advertising, analytics or automatic developer telemetry service is included.

05

Website and support email

This website adds no cookies, tracking scripts or online forms. GitHub Pages hosts it and may process IP addresses and request information. If you email us, we receive your address and the information you choose to send; it is used to respond and investigate, and retained only as needed for support or legal obligations. Your email provider and our email provider process these messages.

06

Your controls and contact

Use Unpair in the Mac device menu to remove a pairing, and clear browser site data to remove its local identifiers. You can revoke Accessibility permission in macOS System Settings. Uninstalling may leave preferences or the Keychain trial timestamp; it does not erase Apple purchase history or restart a trial. For questions or requests to access, correct or delete support correspondence, email peihao78@gmail.com. We will update this page when practices change.

NUMKB / MAC + WEB

Support

Privacy and help for NumKB’s Mac app and Web numeric keypad.

Updated 12 October 2026

Start in four steps

  1. 01

    Connect your Mac and phone to the same trusted Wi-Fi network. Open NumKB on the Mac.

  2. 02

    Scan the QR code in the Mac menu with your phone camera, then open the link in a browser.

  3. 03

    In NumKB Settings → Permissions, grant macOS Accessibility access and check the status again.

  4. 04

    In Settings → NumKB Pro, start the 7-day free trial or buy Pro, then focus the Mac app where you want to enter numbers.

Common questions

The phone cannot connect

Keep NumKB open, confirm both devices use the same network, and scan a fresh QR code. Guest Wi-Fi, VPNs or network isolation can prevent a connection. Check the port in Connection settings.

Connected, but input does not work

Check Accessibility permission and trial/Pro status on the Mac. Select the destination app and an editable field. Restore purchases with the same Apple Account if needed.

How does the free trial work?

It starts when Apple confirms your free trial purchase. It lasts 7 days from the original purchase date. Restoring does not restart it. After expiry, new input pauses until you actively buy lifetime Pro; there is no subscription or automatic charge.

Which devices are supported?

The Mac app requires macOS 14 or later. The first release uses the phone’s Web browser. A native iPhone/iPad client is not part of this release.
